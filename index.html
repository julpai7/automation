Questions

Before you ask

Do I need to change my tools or software?

No. I build on what you already use: Gmail or Outlook, Excel or Google Sheets, Notion, WhatsApp, your website and your CRM. Nothing new to learn.

Will the AI send things to my clients without me checking?

Only if you want it to. By default it prepares drafts and you approve them with one click. You decide what goes out automatically.

What about my data and GDPR?

Your data stays in your own accounts. I document exactly what runs where, use EU-hosted tools where possible, and I'm happy to sign an NDA.

What happens if something breaks?

Every build comes with a written guide covering what it does, what can break it and how to fix it. Fixes are included for 14 or 30 days, and the optional maintenance plan covers it after that.

Will I be dependent on you?

No. You own everything I build, and your team is trained to run it. If you never need me again, that's fine.

Will AI replace people on my team?

That's not what I build. The automations take the repetitive part of a job, and training shows your people how to use AI in their own work. The usual result is the same team doing more of what it was hired for.

Is this for small companies or large ones?

Both. A five-person firm usually starts with one automation. A larger company starts with one team or department, then rolls out what works. The calculator above shows the maths for your team size.

Do you work outside Luxembourg?

Yes. Calls and training run remotely or on site, in English, French or Russian. The Luxembourg grant only applies to Luxembourg companies, the prices are the same everywhere.

How fast is it?

The audit happens within a week of your call. A single automation is usually live about two weeks after that, a system in three to four. If you use the Luxembourg grant, the application step comes first.
